# Landscape printing and priority topics

## What will change
- Print the displayed week in A4 landscape with a wider multi-column layout.
- Recalculate print scaling against the landscape printable area and keep a one-page safety margin.
- In topic selection, let students mark up to three of their selected topics as priorities for each subject.
- Clearly show priority count and prevent a fourth priority until one is removed.
- Schedule priority topics first for that subject, then continue through the remaining chosen topics.
- Show the priority total in the final review.

## Technical details
- Priority selections remain part of plan creation and are used only to order generated session topics; no new stored database fields are needed.
- Priorities are automatically removed if their topic is unticked or replaced by a different source.
- Validate the final print output as a single landscape A4 page and check the setup flow at mobile and desktop sizes.
